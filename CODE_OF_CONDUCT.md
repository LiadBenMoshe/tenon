# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions) is expected to be respectful and
constructive.

- Be welcoming. People come with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Be kind in disagreement. Discuss the code and the idea, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Assume good intent and ask before judging.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that do not follow these rules, and may
block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer at liadbenmoshe10@gmail.com.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
